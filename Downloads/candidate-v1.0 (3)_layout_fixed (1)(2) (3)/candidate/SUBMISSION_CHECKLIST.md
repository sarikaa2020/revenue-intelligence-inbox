# Submission checklist

- Working private URL or local app with exact startup steps.
- Editable source or editable no-code project export; no private keys or dependencies folder.
- Organised generated files and machine-readable manifest.
- Five slides as PPTX or PDF explaining your decisions, evidence and trade-offs.
- Short README: how to run, reproduce each replay step, find exports and interpret metrics. Say which analyses were prepared beforehand and which operations run dynamically.
- Three-minute screen recording with your own spoken narration, submitted as one continuous screen capture. Show one useful manager workflow, one difficult routing decision and the duplicate/update test, including a preserved manual correction. Captions may support your narration but cannot replace it. Include the file or an accessible private link.
- Three most important findings, each with a source passage reference and the exact screen, rule or output it influenced. Put these in the five slides or README.
- One deliberately rejected decision, its reason and accepted trade-off, linked to the resulting product choice. Put this in the five slides or README.
- Approximate hours spent, known limitations and unfinished work.

Before submitting: verify a number against source records; trace a recommendation to a passage; repeat the import; preserve a manual route through the update and restart; open your export and check its paths. You do not need a live session or a long prompt history.
